# Image Rights Record

Record every third-party photograph, illustration, trademark, map, media item, or other visible asset used in App Store screenshots.

For each asset, include:

- the screenshot and UI state where it appears;
- creator or rights holder;
- original source URL or internal asset identifier;
- license or written permission;
- attribution requirements;
- the date the rights were verified.

If all visible assets are original or generated entirely by the app, record that fact and identify the responsible source bundle or fixture.
